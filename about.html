About Jasons Real Estate

A locally rooted real estate agency serving Lae and the wider Morobe Province with professionalism, honesty and local knowledge.

Our Story

Jasons Real Estate was established to provide clear, reliable and professional real estate services to the people of Lae and Morobe Province. We understand that buying, selling or managing property is one of the most important decisions our clients make.

Based in Lae, we combine local market knowledge with a commitment to transparent advice and personal service. Whether you are a first-home buyer, an investor, or a property owner looking for management support, we work alongside you at every stage.

Our focus is simple: deliver results that protect and grow the value of your property while making the process as straightforward as possible.
